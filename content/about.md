**Embedded firmware engineer** (B.S. Electrical Engineering). I write firmware for Mass Flow Controllers used in semiconductor process equipment: sensor acquisition, closed-loop control, calibration, and the industrial communication around them. Currently moving from MCU/RTOS work toward embedded Linux and system software.

This site is my working notebook. Three kinds of posts:

- **Measurements** — experiments with scope captures and numbers. Mostly in English.
- **Insights** — things that changed how I think about firmware. Mostly in Korean.
- **Mistakes** — what I got wrong, and why.

The [Skill Map](/skill-map/) says what I've done and how deep; every claim above level 2 links to evidence. The [Projects](/projects/) page is the queue.
